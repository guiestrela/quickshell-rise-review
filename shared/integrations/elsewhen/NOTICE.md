# Elsewhen attribution

The city/time-zone model, globe mathematics, and bundled world/city data in this directory are derived from Elsewhen by Jason Fried:

- Source: https://github.com/omacom/elsewhen
- License: MIT; see `LICENSE`.

Rise-specific adapters and lifecycle boundaries are maintained separately from the upstream Omarchy plugin. The integration does not reuse its `omarchy.elsewhen` IPC target or its plugin-settings storage.
